Welcome to EduTrack!

EduTrack is your all-in-one student management system designed to simplify the way you manage student records. Whether you're an administrator, teacher, or student, EduTrack provides a user-friendly interface to add, view, update, and delete student information with ease.

Our secure authentication system ensures that your data is protected while giving you quick access to your dashboard. With our organized layout and powerful search functionality, managing your students has never been easier.
